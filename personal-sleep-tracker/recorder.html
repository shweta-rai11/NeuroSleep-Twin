Sleep Recorder

Records a breathing-sound envelope (microphone) and movement (motion sensor) all night, on this device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Not a medical device. This does not measure blood oxygen, heart rhythm, or brain activity — the things a real sleep-apnea evaluation needs. It's a personal screening/tracking tool built from sound and movement only. If you suspect sleep apnea, see a doctor about a real sleep study.
Before you start:
1. Plug the phone in to charge
2. Set brightness low, place it face‑up on the nightstand, mic side toward you — not under the pillow
3. Keep this tab open and the screen on (it will try to stay awake automatically)
